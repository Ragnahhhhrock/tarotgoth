Design system

Everything needed to build the tarotgoth mobile web app: the reader, the palette, the type, the components and the voice. Tokens live in css/tokens.css. Components live in css/components.css.

The reader

Vesper
Twenty-something goth. Dressed in black, pale because she avoids the sun, clever, sarcastic, funny in a way that makes you check whether you were just insulted.
Omen
Her black cat. Judges silently. Appears in loading and empty states, never in the reading itself.
Working names
Vesper and Omen are placeholders. Rename in one place: this page and DESIGN.md.
  • Draw her as a flat vector. Black hair, pale skin, oxblood lips, one raised eyebrow, a gilt crescent at the throat.
  • Always show her inside the ogive, the pointed gothic arch.
  • Keep the cat black with gilt eyes. Never give her a collar with a bell.

Bruised ink, bone and one wound. Dark is the only theme. Oxblood is used for fills only. Rose carries text on dark. Gilt is reserved for money and card edges.

Type

Two families. IM Fell English is the voice: wordmark, headings and the reading itself. It is a revival of seventeenth-century printing, so it looks inked rather than typed. Hanken Grotesk is the interface: buttons, labels, captions. Both are self-hosted.

  • Interface text is plain. Buttons, errors and labels never do the bit. Personality lives in loading lines, empty states and the reading.
  • Be specific about the cards. A joke that could attach to any card is a weak joke.
  • Drop the act when it matters. If someone mentions self-harm, abuse, a medical issue or a crisis, stop the sarcasm and respond plainly with care.
  • No predictions about health, money, legal outcomes or death. Entertainment only, said once in the footer.
  • No em dashes, no exclamation marks, no emoji.

Metadata and social images

Built from the same tokens, avatar and fonts as the app. Regenerate with npm run build:social.

og-image.png, 1200 by 630 (1.91:1). Used by Facebook, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, iMessage, Slack, Discord.

twitter-card.png, 1200 by 600 (2:1) for summary_large_image. Key content stays 60px inside every edge so crops do not cut it.

  • Title: under 60 characters, lowercase brand first.
  • Description: under 160 characters, says what the app does.
  • Icons: favicon.svg, favicon.ico, 180px Apple touch icon, 192 and 512 manifest icons. All use the ogive and gilt crescent on Ink.
  • Theme colour is Ink, so the browser bar matches the page.

Accessibility floor

  • Contrast: text is 7:1 or better in every approved pairing. Borders that mark a control are 3:1 or better.
  • Focus: a 3px rose ring on every interactive element, offset so it never touches the content.
  • Motion: one reveal when the reading arrives, one slow pulse while loading. Both stop under reduced-motion.
  • Images: every card photo and card face has a text equivalent in the reading.
  • Camera: never auto-open it. The person taps to start.
