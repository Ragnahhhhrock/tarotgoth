Privacy

You photograph your spread. The photo is shrunk in your browser and sent to Claude, an AI model from Anthropic, which reads the cards and writes the reading. That is the whole journey.

tarotgoth doesn't save your photo, your question or your reading. There are no accounts and no tracking cookies. Our host, Cloudflare, sees basic request data such as your IP address to keep the site running and to limit abuse.

Anthropic handles the photo under its own terms and privacy policy. Don't photograph anything you don't want an AI service to see.

The $5 AUD tip is handled by Stripe. tarotgoth never sees your card details.
